# Dashboard Screenshots

This folder contains screenshots of the completed Telco Customer Churn Analysis Power BI dashboard.

The screenshots provide a visual preview of the dashboard and highlight the key analytical pages included in the project.

## Dashboard Pages

- **home.png** — Home and dashboard navigation page
- **executive-overview.png** — Executive overview of the customer churn situation
- **churn-drivers.png** — Analysis of customer characteristics and services associated with higher churn
- **customer-retention-risk.png** — Analysis of customer retention patterns and retention risk

These screenshots complement the Power BI report and provide a quick visual overview of the completed project.
